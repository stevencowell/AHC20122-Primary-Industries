Fictional scenario

Prepare three training bays for a stocktake. A fictional supervisor asks a learner team to confirm the bay labels, count six marked training items in each of three bays, record any mismatch, and return the completed record by 11:30. Bay C cannot be entered until the supervisor confirms that another class has finished. The supplied times are planning estimates only: brief 10 minutes, checks 35 minutes, record review 15 minutes.

Boundary: The workplace's real priorities, forms and escalation channel remain controlling.
