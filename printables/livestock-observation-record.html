Fictional scenario

A fictional group approaches a supervised yard transfer. Before movement, the group is standing together near the shaded side of the yard. One animal repeatedly turns its head toward a loose sheet of material moving in the wind. During the teacher-controlled rehearsal, the group spreads and the same animal increases its pace. The supervisor pauses the rehearsal. No animal is handled through this page.
