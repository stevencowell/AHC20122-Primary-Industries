Fictional scenario

Overnight rain changed the training-farm entry. No work is under way. From outside the marked training area, a learner can see a fresh rut across the east lane, a portable pedestrian barrier no longer covering its marked position, and an unfamiliar parked machine partly narrowing the signed walking route. The current local risk matrix and reporting channel are deliberately not supplied.

Boundary: Treat every detail as fictional. Inspect and reason; do not enter, move equipment or recreate the scene.
