Fictional scenario

Four fictional traceability records

A desk-based exercise compares observation cards with an enterprise record extract. No animal, device or marking equipment is present. Identifier formats and numbers are fictional and are not a regulatory example.

Use this boundary: Approved devices, legal requirements, restraint, marking technique and equipment use require the current enterprise source and direct supervision.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

Use the supplied fictional stimulus. Substitute local material only when authorised, privacy-safe and free of controlled assessment content.

Observe

Listen for evidence, source choice, uncertainty and authority boundaries—not just the selected answer.

Debrief

Ask one learner to defend a decision, one to identify an assumption and one to name the next authorised source or person.

Boundary: No device-specific application, restraint or marking technique is taught. Completion creates a private supplementary-learning record only; it does not authorise work, replace workplace records, submit evidence to the RTO or determine competency.
