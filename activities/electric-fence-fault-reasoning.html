Fictional scenario

An offline training diagram has three visible clues

The diagram represents a de-energised classroom model only. It shows a conductor resting against vegetation, a visibly cracked insulator symbol and a gate-handle symbol on the ground. No real fence, energiser or test equipment is present. The system design and manufacturer instructions are not supplied.

Quality check

What a strong practice record shows

Success criteria

  • Maps component relationships at concept level
  • Treats system status as unknown unless authoritatively confirmed
  • Chooses exclusion and reporting rather than touch or test
  • Provides an evidence-rich handover without repair advice

Support

Use SAFE: stay clear, alert others, factual observation, escalate through the current process.

Extension

Explain why an observable clue can be useful even when it does not prove the fault cause.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

Use the supplied fictional stimulus. Substitute local material only when authorised, privacy-safe and free of controlled assessment content.

Observe

Listen for evidence, source choice, uncertainty and authority boundaries—not just the selected answer.

Debrief

Ask one learner to defend a decision, one to identify an assumption and one to name the next authorised source or person.
