Fictional scenario

A fictional wash area sits near a stormwater path

A training map shows loose material beside an uncovered wash area, a fall in the ground toward a stormwater grate, and a drainage line that connects to a farm dam. Three weekly water-meter readings for the wash area are 420, 390 and 440 fictional units. The local spill, waste and notification procedures are not supplied.

Use this boundary: The scenario supports pathway and measurement reasoning only; it is not a spill-response instruction.

Quality check

What a strong practice record shows

Success criteria

  • Traces source, transport, route and receptor
  • Calculates a comparable baseline
  • Separates plausible pathway from proof
  • Proposes a measurable, authorised improvement

Support

Use PATH: potential source, action or transport, travel route, habitat or receptor.

Extension

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

Use the supplied fictional stimulus. Substitute local material only when authorised, privacy-safe and free of controlled assessment content.

Observe

Listen for evidence, source choice, uncertainty and authority boundaries—not just the selected answer.

Debrief

Ask one learner to defend a decision, one to identify an assumption and one to name the next authorised source or person.

Boundary: Local spill, waste, legal-notification and property-control procedures remain authoritative. Completion creates a private supplementary-learning record only; it does not authorise work, replace workplace records, submit evidence to the RTO or determine competency.
