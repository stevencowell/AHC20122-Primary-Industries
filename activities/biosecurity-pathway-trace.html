Fictional scenario

A contractor vehicle approaches a clean production zone

A fictional vehicle arrives from an off-property paddock after wet weather. Dried and damp soil is visible around a wheel arch and one tool tray. The vehicle is outside the controlled clean zone. The property's entry, clean-down and release rules are not supplied.

Use this boundary: Do not infer a product, clean-down method or release criterion. Those details come only from the current local biosecurity plan and supervisor.

Supplied stimulus

Possible movement pathway

Trace the connection; do not assume contamination is confirmed.

  1. Off-property paddock
  2. Vehicle surfaces and tool tray
  3. Entry point
  4. Clean production zone
  5. Soil, plants, animals or water

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Trace the pathway evidence

Classify each statement using only the fictional scenario.

Soil is visible on a surface that moved from off property

Vehicle remains outside the controlled clean zone

Property plan and release rules are unavailable

Support: Use pathway language: source location, carrier, entry point, receiving zone and receptor.

Sequence the planning fields

Order the reasoning steps. This is not a clean-down procedure.

  1. Confirm the current property plan, boundary and authorised person

  2. Record observable material and accessible inspection locations from outside the zone

  3. Build the work-plan fields from the authorised source

  4. Record completion evidence and refer unresolved material or damage

  5. Obtain the authorised release decision before entry

Support: These are information and decision fields, not operational clean-down instructions.

Resolve the release decision

The learner cannot verify the property criteria.

What is the strongest response?

Support: When criteria are missing, make the gap visible and keep the item on hold.

Build the source-controlled plan outline

Name the fields that the authorised local source must fill.

Support: Do not enter product names, method steps or real property details.

Quality check

What a strong practice record shows

Success criteria

  • Traces a plausible movement pathway
  • Separates observation from diagnosis
  • Uses the current property plan as controlling
  • Keeps unresolved evidence on hold and escalates

Support

Use five pathway questions: where from, carried by what, through which point, into which zone, affecting what?

Extension

Add a second fictional carrier and explain which inspection point could interrupt its pathway, without describing a clean-down method.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

Use the supplied fictional stimulus. Substitute local material only when authorised, privacy-safe and free of controlled assessment content.

Observe

Listen for evidence, source choice, uncertainty and authority boundaries—not just the selected answer.

Debrief

Ask one learner to defend a decision, one to identify an assumption and one to name the next authorised source or person.
