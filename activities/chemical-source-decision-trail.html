Fictional scenario

Plan fields are incomplete before a fictional weed-control discussion

Supplied stimulus

Source categories

Choose the source that controls the question; do not invent its contents.

1

Registered label

Controls product-specific authorised uses, restraints and directions for the actual registered product.

2

Safety Data Sheet

Provides current hazard and safety information for the actual product.

3

Workplace register and local procedure

Controls what is held, local access, records, communication and site process.

4

Authorised identification source

Supports plant identification from observable features before control options are considered.

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Map questions to controlling sources

Choose the strongest source category.

Is this product authorised for the intended use and what restraints apply?

Where is current hazard classification and first-aid information found?

Which chemicals are currently held at the workplace and how is access recorded?

What is the plant, based on visible features and location?

Support: The same task may require several sources; one document does not answer every question.

Recognise stop and report conditions

Choose the strongest response to each missing field.

Plant identity remains uncertain

Current registered label is unavailable

Weather and nearby receptor information are not confirmed

Support: Missing identity, source, condition or authority is a decision point—not an invitation to improvise.

Build the observation and non-operational planning record

Use a fictional plant description only.

Support: Do not enter a product, rate, mixture, calibration, PPE or application step.

Quality check

What a strong practice record shows

Success criteria

  • Maps each question to the controlling source
  • Uses observable features before identification
  • Recognises missing-source stop conditions
  • Keeps every operational chemical detail outside the page

Support

Use the decision trail: identify target, find current source, check local authority, check current conditions, record gaps, stop or refer.

Extension

Explain why a label, SDS and workplace register are complementary rather than interchangeable.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

Use the supplied fictional stimulus. Substitute local material only when authorised, privacy-safe and free of controlled assessment content.

Observe

Listen for evidence, source choice, uncertainty and authority boundaries—not just the selected answer.

Debrief

Ask one learner to defend a decision, one to identify an assumption and one to name the next authorised source or person.

Boundary: No product choice, rate, mixing, calibration, PPE, first aid, spill, disposal or application procedure is supplied. Completion creates a private supplementary-learning record only; it does not authorise work, replace workplace records, submit evidence to the RTO or determine competency.
