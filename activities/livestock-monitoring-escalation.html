Quality check

What a strong practice record shows

Success criteria

  • Records changes objectively across time
  • Identifies missing acknowledgement and follow-up fields
  • Escalates through the authorised channel
  • Leaves diagnosis and all treatment details outside the activity

Support

Use the monitoring frame: identifier, time/place, observable cue, comparison, person told, direction, follow-up.

Extension

Write a privacy-safe handover in 40 words or fewer that preserves all essential facts.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

Use the supplied fictional stimulus. Substitute local material only when authorised, privacy-safe and free of controlled assessment content.

Observe

Listen for evidence, source choice, uncertainty and authority boundaries—not just the selected answer.

Debrief

Ask one learner to defend a decision, one to identify an assumption and one to name the next authorised source or person.

Boundary: The activity gives no diagnosis, isolation direction, treatment, dosage, withholding period or euthanasia advice. Completion creates a private supplementary-learning record only; it does not authorise work, replace workplace records, submit evidence to the RTO or determine competency.
