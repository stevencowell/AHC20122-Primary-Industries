Fictional scenario

Coloured counters stand in for feed

This classroom-only exercise uses sealed coloured counters, not feed and not animals. A fictional plan card allocates 8 counters to each of 3 labelled trays. The store card shows 30 counters available. A second record says 26 were issued, so the records do not agree.

Use this boundary: The numbers are deliberately not a ration. No rate, supplement, medicated-feed or livestock-health recommendation is provided.

Quality check

What a strong practice record shows

Success criteria

  • Calculates totals and retains units
  • Finds and preserves the record discrepancy
  • Separates quality observation from feeding recommendation
  • Names the authorised source for all real inputs

Support

Use four checks: source value, operation, units and reasonableness.

Extension

Create a different fictional counter record with one arithmetic error and write a two-sentence reconciliation note.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

Use the supplied fictional stimulus. Substitute local material only when authorised, privacy-safe and free of controlled assessment content.

Observe

Listen for evidence, source choice, uncertainty and authority boundaries—not just the selected answer.

Debrief

Ask one learner to defend a decision, one to identify an assumption and one to name the next authorised source or person.

Boundary: No ration, rate, supplement, medicated-feed or livestock-health recommendation is provided. Completion creates a private supplementary-learning record only; it does not authorise work, replace workplace records, submit evidence to the RTO or determine competency.
