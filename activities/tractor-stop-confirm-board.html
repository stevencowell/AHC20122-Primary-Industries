Task 3 · Formative activity 3

Tractor start, stop or confirm decisions

Use task, terrain, authority, pre-start, traffic and handover evidence to decide start, stop or confirm without learning tractor operation.

30–40 minutesSmall-group board, individual logAutosaves locallyPrintable and downloadable

!

Practice only—not an RTO assessment. These activities create private formative learning records only. They do not replace an RTO assessment task, observation or competency decision. Manufacturer instructions, local SOPs, authorisation, supervision and practical demonstration control tractor operation.

Fictional scenario

Four fictional tractor-readiness cards

Each card describes evidence available before or around a supervised task. Decide whether the evidence supports START, STOP AND REPORT, or CONFIRM BEFORE START. START means only that the planning evidence is aligned; it is not permission to operate.

Use this boundary: No operating, attachment, isolation, testing or maintenance procedure is provided.

Materials

  • Four fictional readiness cards
  • Decision log
  • Linked Task 3 theory modules

Supplied stimulus

Readiness cards

Judge the evidence and authority gap, not machine technique.

A

Aligned plan

The authorised supervisor has matched the routine task, tractor, dry level training area and learner supervision. The current pre-start record is complete and no change is observed.

B

Visible concern

Before entry, the learner sees fresh fluid beneath the parked tractor. The source and serviceability are unknown.

C

Changed terrain

Rain has softened part of the planned route since the brief. No revised route or authority has been confirmed.

D

Shared area

Two pedestrians enter the marked work area while the supervised task is under way.

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Make the four decisions

Choose the strongest field-level decision.

A · aligned plan

B · fresh fluid

C · changed terrain

D · pedestrians in work area

Support: Start is a planning decision only. Any unresolved defect, exposure or changed condition stays with the authorised process.

Choose the record and handover evidence

Do not diagnose or prescribe a repair.

Which note best records Card B?

Support: Record what was seen, where, when, task status and who was told.

What belongs in a supervised usage handover?

Support: A handover preserves status and evidence; it does not prove competence.

Complete the four-card decision log

For each card, record evidence, decision, report and next authorised check.

Support: Do not add operating steps, fault tests or repair instructions.

Quality check

What a strong practice record shows

Success criteria

  • Matches task, terrain, authority and condition evidence
  • Stops for unresolved exposure or defect evidence
  • Uses objective defect wording
  • Keeps all operation and service decisions with authorised sources

Support

Use the board test: authorised task, suitable match, current condition, people separated, record ready.

Extension

Write one new fictional readiness card where the correct answer is CONFIRM BEFORE START and justify the missing evidence.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

Use the supplied fictional stimulus. Substitute local material only when authorised, privacy-safe and free of controlled assessment content.

Observe

Listen for evidence, source choice, uncertainty and authority boundaries—not just the selected answer.

Debrief

Ask one learner to defend a decision, one to identify an assumption and one to name the next authorised source or person.

Boundary: Manufacturer instructions, local SOPs, authorisation, supervision and practical demonstration control tractor operation.
