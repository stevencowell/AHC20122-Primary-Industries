Fictional scenario

Prepare three training bays for a stocktake

A fictional supervisor asks a learner team to confirm the bay labels, count six marked training items in each of three bays, record any mismatch, and return the completed record by 11:30. Bay C cannot be entered until the supervisor confirms that another class has finished. The supplied times are planning estimates only: brief 10 minutes, checks 35 minutes, record review 15 minutes.

Use this boundary: The workplace's real priorities, forms and escalation channel remain controlling.

Supplied stimulus

Supervisor brief

Extract facts before sequencing the work.

  • Purpose: produce an accurate training stocktake record.
  • Dependency: Bay C requires supervisor confirmation before entry.
  • Quality check: label and count must agree; mismatches are recorded, not guessed.
  • Handover: state what was completed, what remains and who was notified.
  • Privacy: use the fictional bay codes only.

Interactive practice

Complete the job

Sequence the plan

Assign each action a unique position from 1 to 5.

  1. Clarify purpose, deadline, roles and the Bay C dependency

  2. Allocate the authorised checks and record owner

  3. Complete accessible checks and record evidence

  4. Seek the supervisor's Bay C confirmation before that check

  5. Review the record and give a concise handover

Support: Look for dependencies and hold points, not just a list of jobs.

Repair the incomplete record

What is the most important problem with this entry?

Support: Ask whether another worker could tell what was checked, what remains and who was told.

Which handover is strongest?

Support: A handover should answer: done, remaining, issue, person told, next confirmation.

Quality check

What a strong practice record shows

Success criteria

  • Extracts purpose, constraints and deadline
  • Sequences a real dependency
  • Checks totals with units
  • Produces an accurate handover without guessing

Support

Use five headings: purpose, people, sequence, checks and handover.

Extension

Add one realistic contingency for a delay, then state who must confirm the change.

Teacher delivery note

Set-up

Use the supplied fictional stimulus. Substitute local material only when authorised, privacy-safe and free of controlled assessment content.

Observe

Listen for evidence, source choice, uncertainty and authority boundaries—not just the selected answer.

Debrief

Ask one learner to defend a decision, one to identify an assumption and one to name the next authorised source or person.
