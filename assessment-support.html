Know which system controls the next step

This page helps you find the current authorised source. It does not copy formal tasks, accept submissions, record work-placement hours or decide competency.

RTO competency

A qualified assessor uses the authorised RTO process and a range of evidence. Website completion cannot make that judgement.

HSC examination

The external HSC examination reports achievement against examination standards. It is separate from RTO competency and qualification documentation.

Work placement

The 240-hour course requires 70 hours recorded through the authorised school/RTO process. Site activities are not placement-hour evidence.

  1. Open the current authorised system named by your teacher or RTO.
  2. Check that the course, unit and current notification match what you were directed to use.
  3. Follow only the instructions and support information in that authorised source.
  4. Use that same system to check receipt, feedback or status; this website cannot confirm them.
