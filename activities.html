Task-authentic formative practice

Practise a real decision, not a worksheet-shaped gap

Each activity uses fictional or de-identified evidence, gives specific feedback, saves privately on this device and links directly to its four taught lessons.

Not formal assessment: activity completion does not prove practical performance, work-placement hours or competence.

Inspect a fictional farm scene, classify hazard and possible harm, then choose a hierarchy-aligned action and reporting path with option-specific feedback.
