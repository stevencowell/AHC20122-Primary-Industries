Before the first day

Teacher/RTO to confirm: host and suitability, dates, contacts, absence and emergency routes, travel, insurance, official hours record and required documentation.

Student check

  • I know who to contact and how.
  • I know what is outside my role or training.
  • I know what information and images must stay private.
  • I have one realistic learning goal.

Ask at induction

  • How are hazards, incidents and equipment faults reported?
  • Which tasks and areas are within my current authorisation?
  • Who checks work and gives feedback?
  • What changes require the task to stop or be referred?
