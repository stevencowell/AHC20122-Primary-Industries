Validated media

Watch with a purpose

Fourteen current Australian-authority or credible clips support 23 lessons. The other 29 lessons use genuine purpose-built visual equivalents because a public clip would add risk or little teaching value.

Privacy by default: no YouTube iframe or remote poster loads until you press Load video. Local procedures and product directions never come from these clips.
