Privacy boundary

Use fictional or generalised reflections

The reflection boxes are for short personal learning notes, not workplace, health, treatment or operating records.

  • Do not enter names or student identifiers.
  • Do not enter property identifiers or confidential workplace details.
  • Do not enter medical, injury or real incident details.
  • Do not enter real animal diagnosis, treatment or withholding records.
  • Do not enter product, rate, mixing, calibration or chemical-use directions.
  • Do not enter tractor, machinery, fencing or electrical operating procedures.
